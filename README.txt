Did your home come with a ventilation unit that was once run by a Kardia smart-home hub, and now refuses to listen? This app lets Homey take over. It talks directly to the network adapter on your ventilation unit, entirely on your local network, without a cloud service or a Kardia account.

Switch between Away, Home and Boost, adjust the temperature, and keep an eye on indoor and outdoor temperature, humidity, CO2 and fine dust. Combine it with your other Homey devices: boost the ventilation when the air gets stuffy, or turn it down when everyone leaves the house.
